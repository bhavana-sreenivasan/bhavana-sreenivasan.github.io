Automated Bone Fracture Detection and Localization

YOLOv8, Python, OpenCV, ONNX, TensorRT, Gradio

Live demo View on GitHub

An object detector that finds and localizes fractures in X-rays, deployed as an interactive demo.

0.52mAP@0.5 (3 seeds, mean ± std)
0.71precision
82 FPSTensorRT FP16 deployment
12.1 mslatency vs 16.1 ms in PyTorch
  • Fine-tuned YOLOv8 on the FracAtlas annotated split (717 boxed X-rays), training at 1024px because over 90% of boxes cover under 2% of the image.
  • Handled class scarcity with 1:1 true-negative background boosting and 3x oversampling of fracture images.
  • Showed that CLAHE plus denoising lowered mAP@0.5 (0.457 vs 0.517). The bottleneck was the small number of unique fracture examples (574 of 4,000+ images), not the architecture, and 91% of misses were hairline fractures.
  • Benchmarked PyTorch, ONNX and TensorRT exports and shipped a Gradio demo on Hugging Face Spaces (ZeroGPU).

Marine Carbonate Prediction: Temporal Validation Case Study

Python, XGBoost, CatBoost, LightGBM, scikit-learn, PyCO2SYS

View on GitHub

A study of why regression models that score well in cross-validation fail on future data.

0.986R² in random CV
0.34R² on 2020–2024 holdout
6regression models compared
0.074R² inflation from a leaky feature
  • Compared six regression models on SOCAT data and showed that random cross-validation overstated performance.
  • Ran four diagnostic experiments: a linear baseline (R² 0.04), latitude and recency-restricted training (0.26) and a hybrid linear plus tree model (0.29). The failure traced to entangled variable interactions, not a single cause.
  • Found and removed a leakage feature (Pressure_equi) that had inflated an earlier version's R².
